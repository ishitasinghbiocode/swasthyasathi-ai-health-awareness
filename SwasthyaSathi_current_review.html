PAUSE BEFORE YOU SHARE

Health Claim Explorer

Practise checking health messages. A viral post or confident statement is not proof.

EXAMPLE 01

“A natural product is always safe because it is natural.”

Is this claim reliable?

Correct: natural does not automatically mean safe. Ingredients, dose, interactions, and quality can matter. Check reliable evidence and consult a qualified professional before using a product for treatment.
EXAMPLE 02

“A message forwarded many times must be medically correct.”

What should you do?

Correct: popularity is not proof. Look for the original source, publication date, evidence, and agreement with reliable public-health organisations. If uncertain, do not share it as fact.
